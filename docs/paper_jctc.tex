\begin{suppinfo}
Per-window overlap for the $19$ real BACE1 adjacent-$\lambda$ BAR windows; the sandwich standard
error at the raw versus the effective sample count; the controlled overlap sweep of the closed form
against learned-variance baselines; the Fisher--resistance correspondence between the sandwich
precision and the conductance of a measurement graph; simulated active-learning acquisition policies and the
knowledge-gradient score on gauge and decision-relevant contrasts; structural context for two flagged systems from
public co-crystal structures; per-system curl-leverage and its relation to reproducible
residuals; the change in unsigned error against experimental affinity after guided edge removal;
detection AUC for three flagging rules; the per-system auditable dimension and detectable-shift resolution over the
benchmark, the added ligand pairs that leave no bridge anywhere, and the influence-ranked repair
race against random removal; the visible fraction recomputed on curated per-edge affinities over
the whole benchmark, split by whether the closure test flags the system, with the node-keying
convention settled by structure and the name-keyed value reported alongside; the label-noise floor beneath that fraction, with the
exact annihilation of a per-ligand field by the projector and the per-system shrinkage each
declared noise level would have to supply; the cross-release split of the multi-release systems and
what it does and does not say about between-preparation variance; the architectures and
training budgets of the amortized predictor and the learned-variance baseline, and the pure-null and
reproducible-systematic simulation protocols;
cycle-closure flagging under four per-edge $\sigma$
assignments and across a swept uniform $\sigma$ scale; per-system reduced $\chi^2$ before and
after inflating each system's own bar; the true hit-rate of a trajectory-balance GFlowNet under
three reward functions; a table collecting the effect of the decomposed $\sigma$ across six
downstream tasks; the replicate stability of the flag set, with per-system reduced $\chi^2$,
degrees of freedom and Benjamini--Hochberg adjusted $q$-values tabulated for each of the three
protocol repeats; the out-of-sample behaviour of the calibrated null, both as a prediction of each
system's closure statistic from replicates that do not enter it and as a held-out test of the
edges it selects; conditional calibration and interval sharpness on real out-of-distribution data,
the amortized reward experiment, and the calibrated stopping rule with its assumed-error sweep.
(PDF)
\end{suppinfo}

\section*{Data and Software Availability}
All data used in this work are public. The alchemtest BACE1 and benzene alchemical sets supply the
work samples of Figure~\ref{fig:A}; the OpenFE IndustryBenchmarks2024 replicate set supplies the $1145$
binding edges and their three protocol repeats; the OpenFF protein--ligand benchmark, which
aggregates the FEP+ and Merck data sets, supplies the downstream audit tasks; and ChEMBL supplies
the experimental affinities used for grounding. No free-energy simulation was run for this work;
all alchemical output is taken as published.
Two findings reported here were sent to the maintainers of that benchmark as public issues on its
repository, \url{https://github.com/OpenFreeEnergy/IndustryBenchmarks2024}: the per-edge
auditability and detectable-shift tables of Section~\ref{sec:decomp} as issue $331$ on 9 August
2026, and the \texttt{cdk8} ligand-name collision of Section~\ref{sec:qc} as issue $332$ on
22 August 2026. On the second the maintainers confirmed the collision, reported that their own
analyses were unaffected by it, and added a note to the release's analysis documentation recording
that ligand names are not unique across data sets; the first remains open.
The BAR layer, the cycle-closure quality-control module, the theorem-verification and
quality-control unit tests, and every figure-regeneration script are released as open source under
a permissive licence, with each figure in this article regenerated by a single documented command.
The code is available at \url{https://github.com/doctawho42/fep-surrogate-pipeline} under the
MIT licence; the version from which every figure in this article was generated is tagged
\texttt{v1.3.0}.
The archive is deposited at \url{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.22046692}, a concept identifier
that resolves to the most recently deposited version.
