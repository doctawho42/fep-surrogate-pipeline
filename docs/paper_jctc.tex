\section*{Data and Software Availability}
All data are public: the alchemtest BACE1 and benzene alchemical sets, the OpenFF
protein--ligand benchmark (which aggregates the FEP+ and Merck data sets), the OpenFE
IndustryBenchmarks2024 replicate set, and ChEMBL (the \texttt{eg5}/KIF11 affinities). The BAR
layer, the surrogate, the cycle-closure quality-control module (\texttt{src/bar/qc.py}), the
theorem-verification and quality-control unit tests, and every figure-regeneration script are
released as open source; each figure is reproduced by a single command (for example,
\texttt{make figL}).

\begin{suppinfo}
Per-edge overlap for the $19$ real BACE1 binding edges; the sandwich standard error at the raw
versus the effective sample count; simulated active-learning acquisition policies and the
knowledge-gradient score on gauge and decision-relevant contrasts; a ligand-only reverse
target-screening case study on ChEMBL data; even and parity-odd readout values on enantiomer
pairs, and the chirality readout on thalidomide; structural context for two flagged systems from
public co-crystal structures; per-system curl-leverage and its relation to reproducible
residuals; the change in unsigned error against experimental affinity after guided edge removal;
detection AUC for three flagging rules; cycle-closure flagging under four per-edge $\sigma$
assignments and across a swept uniform $\sigma$ scale; per-system reduced $\chi^2$ before and
after inflating each system's own bar; the true hit-rate of a trajectory-balance GFlowNet under
three reward functions; a table collecting the effect of the decomposed $\sigma$ across six
downstream tasks; the replicate stability of the flag set, with per-system reduced $\chi^2$,
degrees of freedom and Benjamini--Hochberg adjusted $q$-values tabulated for each of the three
protocol repeats; the out-of-sample behaviour of the calibrated null, both as a prediction of each
system's closure statistic from replicates that do not enter it and as a held-out test of the
edges it selects; conditional calibration and interval sharpness on real out-of-distribution data,
the amortized reward experiment, and the calibrated stopping rule; and the chirality-completeness
result, with its theorem, its enantiomer-pair readouts and its instantiation on thalidomide.
(PDF)
\end{suppinfo}
